Our Foundation

Combining Experience
with Modern Excellence

Ortulex Law Firm (Partners and Associates) was established in January 2023 and is headquartered at D Block, Central Market, 5, First Floor, Sarda Chamber II, Prashant Vihar, Rohini, New Delhi, Delhi 110085. Founded by advocates who bring varied and complementary backgrounds, our firm offers comprehensive legal solutions across jurisdictions.

We practice at every possible forum — from the Supreme Court of India and Delhi High Court, to High Courts in Mumbai, district courts in Delhi-NCR, National and State Consumer Commissions, the National Company Law Tribunal (NCLT), its appellate body (NCLAT), and other specialised forums.

Our ultimate goal is to serve our clients diligently and to secure justice within the bounds of law. We listen carefully, plan strategically, and execute with meticulous attention to detail.
